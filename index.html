What we build

Four structures. One aluminum discipline.

Every system starts from the same slimline aluminum frame — the difference is what sits on top of it: louvers, glass, or fabric.

01 / LOUVER

Bioclimatic Pergolas

Motorized aluminum louvers rotate from full shade to full sky in seconds, with rain sensors, wind-safety retraction and integrated LED.

02 / GLASS

Glass Rooms & Verandas

Structural glazing on a slimline frame, engineered for load and thermal movement, turning a terrace into a room you can use all year.

03 / FABRIC

Retractable Roof Systems

Fabric and PVC roofs on a sliding track for balconies, rooftops and terraces, closing flat against the building when not in use.

04 / COMMERCIAL

Restaurant & Café Canopies

Glazed shopfronts, awnings and covered terraces built for daily service — heavy footfall, quick open-close cycles, street-side wear.

How a project runs

Three stages, in this order.

No stage starts until the one before it is signed off — fabrication doesn't begin until your drawing is confirmed.

01

Survey & Design

We measure on site, model the structure to your load and wind zone, and confirm every dimension with you before anything is cut.

02

Fabrication

Aluminum profiles are cut, powder-coated and pre-fitted in-house, glazing and roof fabric matched to the final drawing.

03

Installation & Handover

Our crew installs on site, tests every motor, sensor and seal, and walks you through operation before we leave.
